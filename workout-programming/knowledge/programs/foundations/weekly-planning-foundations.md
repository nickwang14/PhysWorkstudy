# Weekly Planning Foundations

## Why This Matters
Weekly planning is not about squeezing in the perfect number of workouts. It is about creating a sustainable pattern that supports progress, recovery, and the user's real life.

## Core Ideas
- Workouts should match a realistic frequency target
- Recovery and consistency are central to progress
- A flexible schedule is not a sign of poor discipline; it is a training design requirement
- Deload weeks are intentional periods of reduced fatigue, not failure or inactivity

## Design Principle
Every workout plan should support the user's goal while also acknowledging time constraints, stress, sleep, and life obligations.
