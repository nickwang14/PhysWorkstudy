# Chapter 10: Advanced Weekly Training Planning and Structure

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter builds more complex weekly plans for people who train under real-life constraints. It explores mixed goals, variable schedules, and how to keep a plan coherent when the week changes.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- build a weekly plan for a mixed or shifting schedule
- compare split structures across goal types
- preserve a training pattern during low-time or travel weeks
- sequence sessions intelligently under constraint
- keep the plan consistent without making it rigid

## Subchapters
1. Mixed-goal planning
2. Split architecture trade-offs
3. Travel and low-time weeks
4. Session sequencing under constraints
5. Maintaining consistency during disruption

This chapter unlocks when the learner can explain why a weekly plan should adapt to life constraints without losing the program's main structure.
