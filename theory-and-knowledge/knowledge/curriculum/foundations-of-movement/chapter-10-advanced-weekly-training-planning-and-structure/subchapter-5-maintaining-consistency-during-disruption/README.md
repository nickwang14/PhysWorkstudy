# Maintaining Consistency During Disruption

## Overview
This lesson focuses on preserving the training habit when the user cannot follow the normal plan. The goal is to keep the system alive, not force a perfect week.

## Key Ideas
- disruption is normal
- consistency can survive reduced volume or simplified sessions
- maintaining the habit matters more than preserving every detail
- the plan should support return, not guilt

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter reinforces the app's consistency-first design.

## Knowledge Check
Why is it better to preserve the habit pattern during disruption than to stop completely?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 5 maintaining consistency during disruption. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
