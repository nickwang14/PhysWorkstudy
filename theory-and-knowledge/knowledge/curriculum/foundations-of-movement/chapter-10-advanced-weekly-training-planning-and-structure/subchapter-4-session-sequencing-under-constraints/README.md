# Session Sequencing Under Constraints

## Overview
When time is limited, the order of sessions matters. Sequencing decides what gets priority, what is paired, and what can wait.

## Key Ideas
- session order can affect recovery and output
- the week should place the highest-value work where it fits best
- sequencing helps reduce wasted effort
- good sequence design keeps the user from feeling lost when a day is missed

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to explain why a plan is ordered the way it is, not just what exercises are included.

## Knowledge Check
Why can sequencing matter as much as exercise choice in a constrained week?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 4 session sequencing under constraints. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
