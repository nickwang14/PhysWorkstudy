# Travel and Low-Time Weeks

## Overview
Real users miss time. This lesson teaches how to keep the plan useful when the week is compressed by travel, work, or life constraints.

## Key Ideas
- a shortened week can still be productive
- reduced plans should preserve the goal and the habit
- travel weeks are a planning problem, not a failure
- a good system makes re-entry easy

## Connection to Practice
This lesson reflects the core product idea that flexibility is part of the training system.

## Knowledge Check
What is the main goal of a travel-week plan: perfection or preserving consistency?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 3 travel and low time weeks. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
