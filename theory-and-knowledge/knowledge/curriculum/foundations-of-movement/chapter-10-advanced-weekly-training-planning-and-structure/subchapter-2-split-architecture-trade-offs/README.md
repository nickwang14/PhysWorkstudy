# Split Architecture Trade-Offs

## Overview
Advanced weekly planning often comes down to choosing between split structures. Each structure creates different advantages and trade-offs for recovery, frequency, and user adherence.

## Key Ideas
- split design changes how often muscles or patterns are repeated
- some splits favor recovery while others favor frequency
- the best split depends on the user's schedule and goal
- the wrong split can make a good program harder to follow

## Connection to Practice
The learner should understand that split architecture is a design choice, not a fixed truth.
