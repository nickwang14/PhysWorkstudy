# Coaching for Consistency Over Intensity

## Overview
The final lesson in the foundations path reinforces the product’s central principle: training quality grows from consistency, not constant intensity.

## Key Ideas
- the best training plan is the one a user can repeat over time
- smart coaching reduces guilt, unnecessary pressure, and random plan changes
- consistency produces signal over time, while intensity often creates noise
- a healthy program should teach the user how to adapt without losing momentum

## Connection to Practice
This is the capstone idea that turns the curriculum into a product philosophy. The app should teach users to trust a plan that is sustainable and explainable.

## Knowledge Check
If a user is exhausted but still wants to stay consistent, which is more likely to help long term: pushing harder or keeping the plan sustainable and honest?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 5 coaching for consistency over intensity. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
