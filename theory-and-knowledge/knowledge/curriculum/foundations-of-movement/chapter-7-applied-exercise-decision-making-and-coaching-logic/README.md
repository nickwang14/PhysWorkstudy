# Chapter 7: Applied Exercise Decision-Making and Coaching Logic

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter applies the earlier learning to real-world training decisions. It teaches the learner how to make practical choices about exercise selection, recovery, load changes, and coaching explanations without leaning on medical or diagnostic claims.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- justify exercise selection based on goal, movement quality, and recovery fit
- explain when to regress, progress, or hold a load steady
- describe why consistency matters more than intensity in long-term training
- communicate program decisions in clear coaching language
- understand how to stay within safe, non-clinical decision boundaries

## Subchapters
1. Exercise selection logic
2. Regression, progression, and load adjustment
3. Explaining program decisions
4. Safety boundaries and non-medical judgment
5. Coaching for consistency over intensity

## Unlock Rule
This chapter is the capstone of the foundations path. It unlocks only after the learner can explain and apply program decisions in a way that matches recovery, goals, and schedule constraints.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 7 applied exercise decision making and coaching logic. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
