# Safety Boundaries and Non-Medical Judgment

## Overview
The app should help users make reasonable training decisions within a non-clinical, educational scope. It should not imply medical diagnosis, treatment, or rehab specialization.

## Key Ideas
- exercise decisions should be framed as educational guidance, not medical advice
- safety boundaries help the app stay credible and responsible
- useful coaching often means making reasonable adjustments within the user's current plan
- not every issue requires a clinical interpretation

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter protects the app's product integrity and makes the educational framing clear.
