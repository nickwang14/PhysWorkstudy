# Explaining Program Decisions

## Overview
A good coach can explain why a program is built the way it is. This skill matters because users are more likely to stay engaged when they understand the plan and can connect each decision to a goal.

## Key Ideas
- clear explanations reduce confusion and improve adherence
- good decision-making is as much about communication as mechanics
- users should understand not only what they are doing, but why they are doing it
- the app should support coaching language that is educational and calm

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter ensures the app feels like a learning product, not just a tracker. Users can understand and trust the structure behind their plan.

## Knowledge Check
How would you explain a deload or lighter week to a user who is worried their progress is slipping?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 3 explaining program decisions. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
