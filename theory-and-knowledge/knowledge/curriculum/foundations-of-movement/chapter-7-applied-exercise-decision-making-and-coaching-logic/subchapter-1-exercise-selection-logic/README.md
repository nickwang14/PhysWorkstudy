# Exercise Selection Logic

## Overview
Exercise selection is not just about what looks impressive. It is about which movement best supports the user's goal, the weekly structure, and the movement quality they can repeat safely.

## Key Ideas
- every exercise should have a reason
- exercise choice depends on goal, pattern, recovery, and schedule fit
- simple systems often work better than complicated variation lists
- movement quality matters more than novelty

## Connection to Practice
This chapter turns the earlier theories into an applied framework for choosing meaningful, repeatable work.

## Knowledge Check
If a user wants to build consistency but is tired and busy, why is a simple, repeatable exercise pattern better than a more advanced plan?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 1 exercise selection logic. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
