# Work, Power, Energy, and Momentum

## Overview
These concepts explain how effort is applied over time and how movement can be efficient or costly. For a first-pass curriculum, the goal is not advanced math but a usable understanding of how movement effort interacts with load and performance.

## Key Ideas
- work reflects force applied across a distance
- power reflects how quickly work is done
- energy is the capacity to do work and is influenced by movement speed and load
- momentum connects mass and velocity in motion
- practical training decisions often depend more on control and consistency than maximum output

## Connection to Practice
The learner should know that a movement can be hard because it is slow, heavy, long, or technically demanding. Power and work help explain why different training methods feel different even when the goal is similar.
