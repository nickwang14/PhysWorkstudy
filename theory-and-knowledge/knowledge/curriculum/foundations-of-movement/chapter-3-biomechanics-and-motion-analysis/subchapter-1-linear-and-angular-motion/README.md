# Linear and Angular Motion

## Overview
Human movement is easiest to understand by separating motion into linear and angular components. Linear motion describes movement in a straight line or along a path, while angular motion describes rotation around an axis.

## Key Ideas
- linear motion involves change in position over time
- angular motion involves rotation around a joint or center of mass
- most training movements include both linear and angular components
- understanding motion helps explain squat, hinge, push, pull, and gait patterns

## Connection to Practice
A learner who can recognize whether a movement is mostly linear, angular, or mixed is better equipped to evaluate exercise form and movement quality.
