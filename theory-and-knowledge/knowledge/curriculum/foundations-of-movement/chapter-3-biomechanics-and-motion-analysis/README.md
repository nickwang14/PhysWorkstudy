# Chapter 3: Biomechanics and Motion Analysis

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter introduces the learner to the basic physics of human motion. It explains how bodies move through space, how forces create or resist movement, and how practical exercise decisions can be reasoned about without relying on advanced mechanical calculations.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- describe linear and angular motion in simple terms
- explain the difference between kinematics and kinetics
- identify how force, momentum, and work relate to movement
- connect biomechanics to coaching decisions and exercise quality
- reason about how movement efficiency and stability affect training output
