# Applied Biomechanical Case Studies

## Overview
This final lesson in the chapter applies the motion-analysis concepts to real exercise decisions. It is where the learner practices reasoning through cases rather than isolated definitions.

## Key Ideas
- a case combines movement pattern, load, constraint, and coaching goal
- the same movement can deserve a different cue depending on the scenario
- advanced analysis should still stay practical and user-facing
- case review is the bridge between theory and coaching logic

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to look at a squat, hinge, or carry scenario and explain what is mechanically happening and what the next coaching move should be.

## Knowledge Check
In a case where form breaks down only when fatigue rises, what biomechanical question should come first: "Is the exercise bad?" or "What changed in the constraint?"

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 5 applied biomechanical case studies. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
