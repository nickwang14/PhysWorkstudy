# Chapter 8: Advanced Biomechanics and Motion Analysis

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter deepens the learner's ability to analyze movement by adding more nuanced biomechanics reasoning, constraint-based thinking, and case-based review.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- interpret movement using a more detailed biomechanics lens
- reason about force direction, moment arms, and efficiency trade-offs
- compare movement strategies under different constraints
- connect biomechanics to coaching decisions in harder scenarios
- explain why some movement changes improve performance while others improve control

## Subchapters
1. Movement variability and constraints
2. Force direction and moment arms
3. Simple force-time reasoning
4. Efficiency, control, and trade-offs
5. Applied biomechanical case studies
6. Formal kinematics problem solving
7. Momentum and impulse in exercise movement

## Primary References
- Biomechanics of Human Movement
- Body Physics
- Foundations of Exercise Science

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks when the learner can explain why two movements that look similar may create different mechanical demands and coaching outcomes.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 8 advanced biomechanics and motion analysis. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
