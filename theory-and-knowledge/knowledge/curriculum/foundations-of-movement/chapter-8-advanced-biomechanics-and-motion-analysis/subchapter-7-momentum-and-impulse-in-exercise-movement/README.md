# Momentum and Impulse in Exercise Movement

## Overview
Momentum and impulse help explain how force changes motion across time. These concepts are useful for understanding movement transitions, catching phases, and the difference between controlled motion and abrupt changes.

## Key Ideas
- momentum relates motion to mass and velocity
- impulse is the change in momentum produced over time
- timing of force application matters in exercise movement
- these ideas help explain transition points and braking demands

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to explain why stopping, redirecting, or catching a load changes the demand on the body.

## Scenario
A user can move a weight up cleanly but struggles to control the transition into the lowering phase. The learner should explain how momentum and impulse affect the movement.
