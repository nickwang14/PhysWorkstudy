# Formal Kinematics Problem Solving

## Overview
This lesson turns motion analysis into a more structured problem-solving task. The goal is still practical, but the learner should be able to reason through speed, direction, displacement, and change over time more carefully.

## Key Ideas
- motion can be described with more than one variable
- speed and position changes matter across a rep or movement phase
- structured problem solving reduces guesswork
- advanced analysis should still stay connected to coaching decisions

## Connection to Practice
This lesson helps the learner move from general movement language into more disciplined biomechanics reasoning.

## Scenario
A coach wants to know why the bottom half of a lift looks slower than the top half. The learner should explain what motion variables to compare and what the result suggests.
