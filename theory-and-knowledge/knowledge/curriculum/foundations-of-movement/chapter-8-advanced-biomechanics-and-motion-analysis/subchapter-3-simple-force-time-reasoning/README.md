# Simple Force-Time Reasoning

## Overview
Force is not only about how much load exists; it is also about how long the body has to manage it. Force-time reasoning helps the learner understand why a movement can become harder when tempo, pause, or speed changes.

## Key Ideas
- force demands change over time
- slower control can increase exposure even when the load is unchanged
- pauses, deceleration, and sticking points create distinct demands
- timing matters for both performance and tissue tolerance

## Connection to Practice
This lesson gives the learner a practical way to reason about difficulty without needing advanced mechanics equations.

## Knowledge Check
Why might a paused rep feel harder than a normal rep even if the load does not change?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 3 simple force time reasoning. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
