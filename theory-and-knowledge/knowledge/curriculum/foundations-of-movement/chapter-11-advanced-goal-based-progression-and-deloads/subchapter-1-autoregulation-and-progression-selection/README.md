# Autoregulation and Progression Selection

## Overview
Autoregulation means adjusting progression based on current signal rather than following a rigid plan regardless of context. It is a key advanced skill for sustainable progression.

## Key Ideas
- current readiness can shape progression choice
- not every session should progress
- autoregulation supports both performance and recovery
- the plan should respond to signal, not ego

## Connection to Practice
This lesson helps the learner explain why a good program may progress more slowly in some weeks and more quickly in others.
