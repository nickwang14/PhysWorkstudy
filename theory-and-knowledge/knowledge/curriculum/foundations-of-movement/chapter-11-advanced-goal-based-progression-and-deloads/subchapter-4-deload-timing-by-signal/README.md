# Deload Timing by Signal

## Overview
Deloads are more useful when they are driven by signal patterns instead of a fixed habit alone. This lesson focuses on timing them for the actual user.

## Key Ideas
- deload timing should reflect stress accumulation
- signal-based timing can prevent unnecessary fatigue
- a deload is a programming choice, not a punishment
- the plan should preserve training momentum while reducing strain

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to defend why a deload belongs now instead of later.

## Knowledge Check
Why is a signal-based deload usually more useful than waiting for complete burnout?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 4 deload timing by signal. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
