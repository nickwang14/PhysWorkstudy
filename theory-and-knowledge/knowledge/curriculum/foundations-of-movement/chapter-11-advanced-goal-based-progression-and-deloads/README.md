# Chapter 11: Advanced Goal-Based Progression and Deloads

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter turns progression into a decision system. It focuses on autoregulation, troubleshooting, and using deloads as a tool for long-term momentum.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- compare progression models
- use signal-based autoregulation
- decide when a stall needs a program change
- time deloads from stress signals rather than habit alone
- preserve momentum while modifying the plan

## Subchapters
1. Autoregulation and progression selection
2. Progression model comparison
3. Stall troubleshooting
4. Deload timing by signal
5. Preserving momentum through change

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks when the learner can explain why progression should respond to signal patterns instead of forcing a rigid increase.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 11 advanced goal based progression and deloads. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
