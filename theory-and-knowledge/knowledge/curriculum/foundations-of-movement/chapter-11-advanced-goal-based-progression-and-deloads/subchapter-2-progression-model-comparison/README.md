# Progression Model Comparison

## Overview
Different progression models work better in different situations. This lesson compares those models so the learner can reason about fit rather than treating one method as universal.

## Key Ideas
- some progression models favor simplicity
- some favor faster adaptation
- some work better when recovery is uncertain
- the best model depends on goal and consistency

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to explain why a model is chosen instead of just copying a template.

## Knowledge Check
Why might two different users need different progression models even if they are doing similar exercises?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 2 progression model comparison. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
