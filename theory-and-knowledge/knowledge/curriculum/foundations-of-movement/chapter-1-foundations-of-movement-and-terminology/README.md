# Chapter 1: Foundations of Movement and Terminology

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter establishes the vocabulary and conceptual foundation needed to understand every later topic in the learning path. It is the first gate in the progression map.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- define movement, force, load, and stability in plain language
- recognize common movement patterns
- understand how joint movement is described
- explain basic principles of muscles and force production
- connect movement terminology to exercise selection and program thinking

## Subchapters
1. Movement terminology
2. Planes and axes
3. Joint and segment movement basics
4. Force, load, and stability
5. Movement patterns and how they appear in training

## Primary References
- Anatomy and Physiology 2e
- Biomechanics of Human Movement
- Foundations of Exercise Science
- Body Physics

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks the next progression gate only after the learner completes the foundational micro-lessons and the concept checks associated with each subchapter.

## Scenario Layer
Use simple training cases to reinforce the terminology:
- identify the movement pattern in a squat, hinge, push, or pull
- explain why two exercises can use similar joint actions but different names
- translate a coaching cue into plain movement language

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 1 foundations of movement and terminology. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
