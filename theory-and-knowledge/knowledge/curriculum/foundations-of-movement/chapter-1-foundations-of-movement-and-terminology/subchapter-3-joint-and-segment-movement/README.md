# Joint and Segment Movement

## Overview
This subchapter introduces the basic ways joints move and the language used to describe the motion of body segments. It helps the learner interpret movement in concrete terms rather than as vague effort or intensity.

## Key Ideas
- body segments move relative to one another
- joint movement is described using standard movement terms
- movement quality depends on the relationship between control, force, and segment position
- better terminology improves program choice and exercise reasoning

## Connection to Practice
Once users can describe how a segment moves, they can better explain a squat, a hinge, a push, or a pull. This makes exercise selection and progression much more understandable.
