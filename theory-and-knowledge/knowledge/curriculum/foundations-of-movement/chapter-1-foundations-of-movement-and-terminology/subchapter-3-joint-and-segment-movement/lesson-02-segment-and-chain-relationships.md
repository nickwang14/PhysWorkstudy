# Segment and Chain Relationships

The body is made of connected segments: trunk, pelvis, thigh, shank, foot, arm, forearm, and hand. These segments do not move independently in most real movement. Instead, they connect and influence one another through joints and through the body’s neuromuscular control.

The idea of a kinetic chain helps explain this relationship. In an open chain, the end of the limb is free to move; in a closed chain, the end of the limb is fixed or grounded. These distinctions matter because the same muscle group can contribute differently depending on whether the body is moving through a stable base or a mobile limb.

## Why It Matters
Understanding segment relationships helps explain why exercise performance depends on coordination as much as on isolated muscle strength. Movement is rarely just one joint moving in isolation.

## Key Terms
- body segment
- kinetic chain
- open chain
- closed chain
- coordinated movement
- stability
