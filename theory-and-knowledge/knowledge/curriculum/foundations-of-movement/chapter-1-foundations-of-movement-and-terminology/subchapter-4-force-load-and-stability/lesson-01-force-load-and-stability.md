# Force, Load, and Stability

Force is any push or pull that can change the motion of a body or segment. Load is the external or internal stress placed on the body as a result of that force. Stability is the ability to control posture and movement under stress.

These are essential ideas in training because good exercise does not just move weight — it resolves force through the body in a controlled way. Too much instability without control can create poor movement quality, while too little challenge may not create meaningful adaptation.

## Key Terms
- force
- load
- stability
- stress
- control
