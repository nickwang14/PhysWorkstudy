# Force, Load, and Stability

## Overview
This subchapter introduces the core ideas behind how the body interacts with stress and control. These concepts bridge movement terminology and training design.

## Key Ideas
- force is a push or pull that changes motion
- load is the stress placed on the system
- stability is the ability to control movement under that load
- training is not just about effort; it is about how well the body can manage force and control

## Connection to Practice
The learner should begin to understand why a workout is not just a series of exercises. It is a sequence of controlled, recoverable stresses designed around movement, loading, and stability.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 4 force load and stability. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
