# Load Types and Muscle Tension

Load is not just “how heavy something is.” It is the way stress is applied to the body. Different loads create different demands. Compression pushes tissues together, tension pulls tissues apart, shear creates sliding forces, and torsion creates twisting stress.

Muscle tension is the internal force generated by muscles to resist or produce movement. This internal force allows the body to manage external load, hold posture, and produce force in a coordinated way. A person can have a heavy external load and still move well if the muscles generate appropriate tension and control.

## Why It Matters
Understanding load types helps the learner appreciate why some exercises feel harder than others even when the weight is similar. The body does not only “handle weight”; it manages force in multiple directions and at multiple joints.

## Key Terms
- compression
- tension
- shear
- torsion
- muscle tension
- internal force
