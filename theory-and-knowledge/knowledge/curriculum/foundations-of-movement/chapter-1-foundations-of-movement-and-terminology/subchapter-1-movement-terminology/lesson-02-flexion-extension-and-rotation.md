# Flexion, Extension, and Rotation

Three of the most common terms used to describe human movement are flexion, extension, and rotation. These terms describe how a body segment moves relative to a joint or a body plane.

- Flexion usually describes movement that decreases the angle between body segments.
- Extension usually describes movement that increases the angle between body segments.
- Rotation describes movement around an axis or along a longitudinal direction.

These terms are foundational because they allow the learner to describe movement precisely. Without them, it is hard to reason clearly about exercise technique, joint function, and movement quality.

## Key Terms
- flexion
- extension
- rotation
- joint angle
- axis
