# Anatomical Position and Reference Terms

Anatomical position is the standard reference posture used in anatomy and movement science. In this position, the body stands upright, the feet are parallel, the arms are at the sides, and the palms face forward. This standardized starting position allows professionals to describe movement clearly and consistently.

Reference terms help us describe where body parts are in relation to each other. For example, medial means toward the midline, lateral means away from the midline, proximal means closer to the trunk, and distal means farther from the trunk. Superior describes a structure above another, while inferior describes one below it.

## Why It Matters
Without a common reference system, movement descriptions become vague and inconsistent. Anatomical terminology gives us a shared language for describing direction, position, and joint movement.

## Key Terms
- anatomical position
- medial
- lateral
- proximal
- distal
- superior
- inferior
- anterior
- posterior
