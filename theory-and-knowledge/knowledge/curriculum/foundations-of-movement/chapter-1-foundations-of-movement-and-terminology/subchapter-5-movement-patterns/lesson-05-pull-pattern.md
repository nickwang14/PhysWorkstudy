# Pull Pattern

The pull pattern describes movement where the body or a segment moves toward the midline or creates force by drawing a load toward the body. This includes rows, pulldowns, and many single-arm pulling patterns.

Pull work is often discussed as “back training,” but from a movement perspective it is better understood as a pattern that trains force control, shoulder stability, and trunk integration. A pull pattern is not simply “good posture training”; it is one of many useful movement systems that should be balanced with pushing, hinging, and squatting patterns.

## Why It Matters
Pull patterns help underlie posture, upper-body control, and the ability to manage load in a balanced way. Training that neglects pull work entirely often creates an imbalance in the way force is produced and controlled through the upper body.

## Key Terms
- pull pattern
- rowing
- scapular positioning
- shoulder control
- trunk integration
