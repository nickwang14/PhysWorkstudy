# Carry Pattern

The carry pattern is a loaded movement where the user holds a load in a position that requires trunk stability and postural control while the limbs move or while the body travels through space. Carries are often used because they build control, alignment, and full-body tension.

A carry is not only a “must-do” exercise. It is a way of training the body to maintain alignment and control under an external load. This is incredibly valuable because real-life strength depends as much on stability and coordination as on raw force production.

## Why It Matters
Carries help learners understand that movement quality is not limited to isolated exercises. Training the body to hold and manage load while the torso and limbs stay coordinated is important for daily function and sport performance.

## Key Terms
- carry pattern
- posture
- trunk stability
- controlled loading
- full-body tension
