# Squat Pattern Deep Dive

The squat pattern is one of the most fundamental human movement patterns. It involves a coordinated bend through the hips and knees while maintaining balance and control. It appears in many forms: bodyweight squats, goblet squats, back squats, front squats, split squats, and other lower-body exercises.

A squat pattern is not just a strength exercise. It is a way of understanding how the body manages load, stability, and mobility through a broad range of motion. The key is not only how much one can squat, but how well the body can control the pattern under load.

## Why It Matters
The squat pattern appears in daily life and training. Sitting down, standing up, lifting an object, and controlling a descent all depend on a stable, coordinated squat pattern. This makes it a foundational concept in both movement competence and training design.

## Key Terms
- squat pattern
- hip flexion
- knee flexion
- trunk control
- lower-body stability
