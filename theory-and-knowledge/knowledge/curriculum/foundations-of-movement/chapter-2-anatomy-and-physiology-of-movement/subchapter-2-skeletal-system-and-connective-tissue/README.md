# Skeletal System and Connective Tissue

## Overview
The skeletal system gives the body structure, protects vital tissues, and provides levers for movement. Connective tissue helps hold the system together, supports joints, and transmits tension as the body moves.

## Key Ideas
- bones provide structure and storage of minerals
- connective tissue makes movement possible through support and force transfer
- tendon and ligament structures differ in size, role, and behavior
- joint stability depends in part on how tissues are organized and loaded

## Connection to Practice
Exercise quality depends on the body�s ability to move through the skeleton with control. A learner who understands the skeletal system can better understand why alignment, joint position, and load matter.
