# Bone Architecture and Loading

Bone is not a simple solid rod. It has a structure that reflects the demands placed on it. Dense outer surfaces provide strength, while internal architecture helps manage loading and distribute force. This makes bone well suited to both support and movement.

The skeleton is designed to absorb force and provide a stable frame for motion. Different bones have different shapes because they experience different loading patterns. Long bones help with lever-based movement, while broader bones or joint surfaces help distribute weight and guide motion at a joint.

## Why It Matters
Understanding bone architecture helps explain why the body is built to handle load in specific ways. It also helps connect anatomy with exercise mechanics: the structure of the skeleton influences what kinds of movement are efficient and what kinds of positions may create more stress.
