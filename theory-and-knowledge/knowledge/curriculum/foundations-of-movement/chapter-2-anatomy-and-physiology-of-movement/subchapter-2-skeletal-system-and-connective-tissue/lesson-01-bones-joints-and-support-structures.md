# Bones, Joints, and Support Structures

The skeleton acts as the body’s framework. It provides support, protection, and leverage. Bones are the rigid elements that support body segments, while connective tissues such as ligaments and tendons help keep the system stable and functional.

Ligaments connect bone to bone and help maintain joint integrity. Tendons connect muscle to bone and transmit muscle-generated force to the skeleton. These structures are essential because movement is not just muscle pulling in isolation; it is the result of force being transferred across segments and joints.

## Why It Matters
Without understanding how support structures work, a learner may think movement is only about muscles. In reality, bones and connective tissues are essential to the way force is managed and controlled.
