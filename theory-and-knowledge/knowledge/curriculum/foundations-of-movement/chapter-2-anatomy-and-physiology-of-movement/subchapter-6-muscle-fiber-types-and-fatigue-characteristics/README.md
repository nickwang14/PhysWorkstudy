## Overview
This lesson adds a more detailed view of how muscles behave under repeated effort. Different fibers respond differently to speed, load, and fatigue, which helps explain why the same workout can feel different across users.

## Key Ideas
- muscles do not all fatigue at the same rate
- some fibers are better suited to repeated effort, while others contribute more to force and power
- fatigue characteristics affect exercise choice and session structure
- the body recruits muscle resources based on task demand

## Connection to Practice
This lesson helps the learner connect tissue behavior to training design rather than assuming all muscular work is identical.

## Scenario
Two users do the same circuit. One fades quickly in repeated work while the other struggles with higher-force efforts. The learner should explain how fiber behavior and fatigue characteristics might shape the next session.
