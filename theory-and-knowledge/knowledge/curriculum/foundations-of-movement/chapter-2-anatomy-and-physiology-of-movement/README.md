# Chapter 2: Anatomy and Physiology of Movement

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter explains how the body is built to move and how the musculoskeletal system transfers force, stabilizes posture, and creates movement. It is the next progression gate after the movement terminology chapter and helps the learner connect structure to function.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- describe the major tissue types involved in movement
- explain how bones, connective tissue, and muscle work together
- identify major muscle actions and contraction types
- relate joint structure to leverage and force transfer
- connect anatomy to movement quality and practical limitations

## Subchapters
