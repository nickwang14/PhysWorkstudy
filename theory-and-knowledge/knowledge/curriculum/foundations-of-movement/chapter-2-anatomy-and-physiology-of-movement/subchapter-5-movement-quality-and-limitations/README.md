# Movement Quality and Limitations

## Overview
Movement quality describes how well the body controls a movement under load, time, and fatigue. It includes coordination, alignment, control, and the ability to move through intended ranges without unnecessary compensation.

## Key Ideas
- movement quality is more than range of motion or effort
- mobility and stability both matter
- tissue tolerance and fatigue shape what a person can do well
- exercise must match the user�s current movement capabilities

## Connection to Practice
This chapter helps the learner bridge anatomy to real-life training decisions. A good plan does not just push harder � it also respects the body�s current capability and control.
