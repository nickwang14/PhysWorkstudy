# Movement Quality and Practical Limits

Movement quality is the ability to perform a movement with control, alignment, and appropriate effort. It is not the same as “more range” or “more intensity.” A quality movement allows the body to manage force and posture while staying coordinated.

Practical limits come from anatomy, tissue tolerance, current control, fatigue, and context. A person may have the theoretical range of motion to do a movement but not yet have the control, stability, or recovery to do it well. This is why exercise progression is about more than just adding load.

## Why It Matters
This concept helps learners understand why good training is adaptive and individualized. Movement quality gives the practical boundary between what is possible and what is effective at a given moment.

## Key Terms
- movement quality
- control
- alignment
- tissue tolerance

- practical limit
