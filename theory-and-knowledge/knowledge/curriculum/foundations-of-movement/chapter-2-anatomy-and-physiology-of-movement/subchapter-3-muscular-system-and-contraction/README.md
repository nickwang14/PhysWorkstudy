# Muscular System and Contraction

Source, editorial, and domain review are pending for all material in this module.

## Overview
The muscular system is responsible for creating movement, controlling posture, and managing force. It does not act as a single block of tissue; it is a coordinated network of muscles working through contraction and tension.

## Key Ideas
- muscles contract to create force
- different contraction types affect movement differently
- muscular force is shaped by position, joint angle, and control
- muscles work in coordination with the nervous system and skeleton

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter helps explain why the same movement can feel different across positions, loads, and fatigue states. Understanding muscle action supports clearer descriptions of movement, but does not establish an individual's tissue tolerance or prescribe a training dose.
