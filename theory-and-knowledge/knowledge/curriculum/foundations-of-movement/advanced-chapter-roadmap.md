# Advanced Chapter Roadmap

## Purpose
This roadmap defines the next phase after the core Foundations of Movement sequence. The advanced chapters are real curriculum chapters, not optional review overlays.

## Design Rule
- Core chapters remain the MVP-safe foundation.
- Advanced chapters continue the same learning areas with more depth.
- The same lesson planning workflow still applies: initiate, extend, compare with source material, and defer overly specialized topics to the backlog.

## Planned Advanced Chapters

### Chapter 8: Advanced Biomechanics and Motion Analysis
- movement variability
- constraint-based analysis
- force direction and moment arms
- force-time reasoning
- applied coaching case studies

### Chapter 9: Advanced Load, Fatigue, and Recovery
- fatigue monitoring
- cumulative load management
- recovery markers
- adaptation across blocks
- decision points for plan changes

### Chapter 10: Advanced Weekly Training Planning and Structure
- mixed-goal planning
- split architecture trade-offs
- travel and low-time weeks
- session sequencing under constraints
- maintaining consistency under disruption

### Chapter 11: Advanced Goal-Based Progression and Deloads
- autoregulation
- progression model comparison
- stall troubleshooting
- deload timing by signal
- preserving momentum through change

### Chapter 12: Advanced Applied Exercise Decision-Making and Coaching Logic
- decision trees
- case-based programming choices
- advanced coaching language
- consistency-first program synthesis
- real-world plan adjustments

## Backlog Boundary
If a topic becomes too technical, clinical, or research-heavy for the active product direction, it should still move to `project-management/curriculum-backlog.md`.
