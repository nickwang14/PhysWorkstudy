# Constraint-Led Case Reasoning

## Overview
In advanced cases, the learner should reason from the constraint outward: what changed, what is still possible, and what adjustment best preserves the goal.

## Key Ideas
- constraints shape the decision space
- case reasoning starts with what the user can actually do
- the best answer may be a simplification rather than an upgrade
- reasoning should remain practical and non-clinical

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter links biomechanics, load, and planning into a single applied method.
