# Consistency-First Synthesis

## Overview
This final lesson combines the curriculum into a single principle: the best program is the one that can be repeated, explained, and adjusted without losing the user's confidence or momentum.

## Key Ideas
- consistency is the organizing principle
- advanced reasoning should still support repeatability
- the learner should be able to synthesize movement, load, recovery, and planning
- the final goal is practical, explainable coaching

## Connection to Practice
This is the capstone lesson for the advanced curriculum.

## Knowledge Check
Why does a consistency-first plan often outperform a more aggressive but less repeatable one?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 5 consistency first synthesis. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
