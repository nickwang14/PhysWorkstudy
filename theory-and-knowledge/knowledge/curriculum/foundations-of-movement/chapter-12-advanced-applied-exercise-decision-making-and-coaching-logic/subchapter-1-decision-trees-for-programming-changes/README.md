# Decision Trees for Programming Changes

## Overview
Advanced coaching often starts with a decision tree: if this signal appears, then this is the likely adjustment. Decision trees help the learner avoid random changes.

## Key Ideas
- decisions should follow evidence and context
- the same symptom can lead to different fixes
- trees help keep reasoning consistent
- the plan should be explainable from branch to branch

## Connection to Practice
This lesson turns coaching into a repeatable reasoning structure.

## Knowledge Check
Why is a decision tree better than improvising a new change every time something feels off?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 1 decision trees for programming changes. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
