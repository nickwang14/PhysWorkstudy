# Recovery Markers and Trend Signals

## Overview
Advanced recovery management uses signals, not guesses. The learner should understand which markers can help describe whether the body is adapting well or drifting toward overload.

## Key Ideas
- recovery markers can include sleep quality, soreness trend, readiness, and repeated performance
- no single marker tells the whole story
- trend signals matter more than one-day noise
- recovery data should guide decisions, not create obsession

## Connection to Practice
This lesson supports practical decision-making in an app that emphasizes consistency and sustainable progress.

## Knowledge Check
Why is a trend in recovery more useful than a single good or bad day?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 3 recovery markers and trend signals. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
