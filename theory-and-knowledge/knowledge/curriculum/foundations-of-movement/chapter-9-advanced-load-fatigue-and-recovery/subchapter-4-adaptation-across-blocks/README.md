# Adaptation Across Blocks

## Overview
Training adaptation is often easier to understand when seen across blocks of time. This lesson focuses on how repeated exposure, recovery, and progression combine over several weeks.

## Key Ideas
- adaptation is the result of repeated stress and recovery
- blocks help organize stress so the body can respond
- progress may not be obvious inside one session
- adaptation can differ by goal, movement, and recovery capacity

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to explain why a plan can feel hard before it feels successful.

## Knowledge Check
Why is it useful to evaluate adaptation across a block instead of only after each workout?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 4 adaptation across blocks. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
