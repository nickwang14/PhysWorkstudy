# Cumulative Fatigue Patterns

## Overview
Fatigue becomes more important when it accumulates over time. This subchapter focuses on patterns that show up across several sessions or weeks rather than a single workout.

## Key Ideas
- fatigue can build quietly across repeated stress
- cumulative fatigue affects output, mood, and movement quality
- a single good session does not erase a poor recovery trend
- patterns matter more than one-off signals

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to see when a plan is drifting from productive stress into repeated strain.

## Knowledge Check
What is a better indicator of a fatigue problem: one tired session, or several weeks of declining recovery and output?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 2 cumulative fatigue patterns. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
