# Force-Time Reasoning for Coaching Decisions

## Overview
This lesson ties force, timing, and fatigue into a coaching choice. It helps the learner decide when a movement is becoming too costly and what the next adjustment should be.

## Key Ideas
- force demands vary across the duration of a set
- timing changes how effort is experienced
- a useful coaching decision often depends on where the demand appears
- force-time thinking helps explain sticking points and drift in quality

## Connection to Practice
This lesson is where biomechanics becomes a coaching tool for load management and movement quality.

## Scenario
A user performs the first half of a set well, then loses control at the end. The learner should explain whether the problem is total load, force timing, or accumulated fatigue.
