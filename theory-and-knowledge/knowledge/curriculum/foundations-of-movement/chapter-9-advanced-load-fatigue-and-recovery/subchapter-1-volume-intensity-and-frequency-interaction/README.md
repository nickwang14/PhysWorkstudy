# Volume, Intensity, and Frequency Interaction

## Overview
Advanced load management requires seeing training as a relationship between volume, intensity, and frequency rather than a single variable. Changing one usually affects the others.

## Key Ideas
- volume is the amount of work
- intensity is the challenge level of that work
- frequency is how often the work repeats
- the three variables interact across the week and the block

## Connection to Practice
This lesson helps the learner reason about why two programs with the same exercise selection can still create very different fatigue profiles.

## Knowledge Check
Why can a moderate load repeated often sometimes create more fatigue than a heavier load used less often?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 1 volume intensity and frequency interaction. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
