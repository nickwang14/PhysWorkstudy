# Load-Management Case Studies

## Overview
This chapter closes with case-based load management. The learner practices choosing between holding, reducing, or shifting the plan based on real signals.

## Key Ideas
- case review connects theory to planning decisions
- load management is context-dependent
- recovery and output should be reviewed together
- the best decision is usually the one that preserves repeatability

## Connection to Practice
The learner should be able to review a case and explain why a lighter or more structured week is better than pushing through.

## Knowledge Check
If a user is still training but performance is dropping week after week, what load-management response is most reasonable?

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving subchapter 5 load management case studies. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
