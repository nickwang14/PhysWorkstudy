# Chapter 5: Weekly Training Planning and Structure

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter turns the science of movement, recovery, and adaptation into a practical weekly plan. It focuses on schedule flexibility, frequency, and the minimum effective structure needed for consistency.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- explain how training frequency interacts with goal attainment and recovery
- understand why flexible scheduling can support consistency better than rigid rules
- design or interpret a basic weekly plan based on available time and training goals
- recognize the difference between session quality and session count
- connect weekly structure to recovery and long-term adherence

## Subchapters
1. Schedule flexibility and session frequency
2. Exercise selection by goal
3. Session design and weekly structure
4. Rest days and balance
5. Minimum effective consistency

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks when the learner can build a simple weekly training structure that matches their availability and stated goal without creating excessive strain.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 5 weekly training planning and structure. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
