# Chapter 5: Weekly Training Planning and Structure

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter turns the science of movement, recovery, and adaptation into a practical weekly plan. It focuses on schedule flexibility, frequency, and the minimum effective structure needed for consistency.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- explain frequency as one planning variable rather than a universal weekly target
- organize a flexible weekly schedule around realistic availability and a stated goal
- identify the purpose of selected exercises and sessions in a simple weekly plan
- distinguish an individual session's content from the distribution of work across a week
- describe how recovery opportunities and participation barriers can affect a plan's repeatability

## Subchapters
1. Schedule flexibility and session frequency
2. Exercise selection by goal
3. Session design and weekly structure
4. Rest days and balance
5. Minimum effective consistency
