# Rest Days and Balance

## Overview
Rest days are not a sign of laziness or lost momentum. In a sustainable plan, recovery days help the body absorb training stress and keep the next session productive.

## Key Ideas
- rest days protect the quality of the next session
- balance is created by properly spacing work and recovery, not by maximizing daily effort
- movement can still be useful on lighter days, but it should serve recovery rather than create more fatigue
- the best plan makes room for the user's actual life, not just ideal training logic

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter reinforces that the app is designed around consistency and sustainable performance rather than guilt-based daily pressure.
