# Session Design and Weekly Structure

## Overview
A single workout is only one part of a system. The overall weekly structure determines whether the user gets enough work, enough recovery, and enough consistency to progress.

## Key Ideas
- sessions should fit into a repeatable rhythm
- weekly structure reduces decision fatigue and makes training more sustainable
- rest between sessions matters as much as the work itself
- a planned week can adapt to real life without losing the core objective

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter is where the learner begins to see the app as a weekly system instead of a collection of isolated workouts.

## Knowledge Check
If a user can only complete two workouts in a week, what should determine whether those sessions are effective: the total time, the exercise choices, or the recovery structure?

## Scenario
