# Fatigue and Adaptation

## Overview
Fatigue is the normal cost of training. Adaptation occurs when recovery is sufficient to allow the body to improve after that stress is handled well.

## Key Ideas
- fatigue can be local, systemic, or cumulative
- adaptation depends on repeated exposure and recovery, not one perfect effort
- fatigue is not always a sign of failure; it is part of the training cycle
- the goal is to manage fatigue so performance improves without chronic breakdown

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter teaches that sustainable quality matters more than one heroic session. The best program is one that allows the user to keep showing up.

## Knowledge Check
If fatigue is rising each week without improvement, what is the likely issue: the plan is too easy, or the plan is not being recovered well enough?
