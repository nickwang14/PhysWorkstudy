# Chapter 4: Load, Fatigue, and Recovery

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter teaches how training stress creates adaptation, why recovery is necessary, and how the learner should think about fatigue without confusing effort with damage. It turns movement science into a practical understanding of training sustainability.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- distinguish between fatigue, soreness, and training stress
- explain how load interacts with recovery and adaptation
- identify the role of rest and deload periods in a balanced plan
- understand why good training is consistent and repeatable, not permanently maximal
- recognize early warning signs that require a program adjustment

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks when the learner can explain how load, adaptation, and recovery fit into a weekly plan without oversimplifying the relationship.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 4 load fatigue and recovery. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
