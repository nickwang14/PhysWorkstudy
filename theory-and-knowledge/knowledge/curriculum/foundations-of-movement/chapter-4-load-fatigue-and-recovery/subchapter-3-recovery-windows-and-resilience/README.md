# Recovery Windows and Resilience

## Overview
Recovery is not passive rest alone. It is the time and conditions that allow the body to absorb stress and recover function. Recovery windows are what make a training plan sustainable.

## Key Ideas
- sleep, nutrition, stress load, and session quality all affect recovery
- resilience grows from repeatable routines and enough recovery between hard efforts
- good recovery supports movement quality, not just soreness reduction
- the body improves when training and recovery are balanced over time

## Connection to Practice
A learner who understands recovery windows is better prepared to manage flexibility in scheduling, avoid overtraining, and handle busy weeks without panic.
