# Soreness, Stress, and Risk Signals

## Overview
Not all discomfort means the body is failing. It is important to distinguish between normal training stress, soreness, and signs that the plan may be too much or the user may need a pause or modification.

## Key Ideas
- soreness is not the same as injury
- fatigue may be useful, but persistent poor recovery is a warning sign
- program decisions should be based on patterns, not single moments
- risk signals are not a training plan cue; they are a signal to step back and reassess

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter helps the learner avoid overreacting to every discomfort while still respecting signs that the plan needs modification.
