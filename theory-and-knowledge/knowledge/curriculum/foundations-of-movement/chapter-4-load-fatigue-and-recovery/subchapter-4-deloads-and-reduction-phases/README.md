# Deloads and Reduction Phases

## Overview
A deload is a planned reduction in load, intensity, or fatigue. It is not a failure or a break in discipline. It is a deliberate training tool that helps the body absorb the accumulated stress of continued training.

## Key Ideas
- deloads are planned and intentional
- reduction phases help preserve quality and reduce fatigue drift
- some weeks should feel lighter even if the plan still includes movement
- the purpose of deload is to support long-term consistency, not to punish effort

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter is essential because it connects the science of adaptation to the app's weekly or cycle-based training design. It supports the product principle that consistency beats intensity.
