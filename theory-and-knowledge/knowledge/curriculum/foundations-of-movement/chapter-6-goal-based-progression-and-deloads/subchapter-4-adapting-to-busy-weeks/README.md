# Adapting to Busy Weeks

## Overview
A plan should survive real life. A busy week should not mean abandoning the program entirely; it means making a reasonable, goal-based adjustment.

## Key Ideas
- flexibility allows the user to keep the habit alive during busy weeks
- a reduced week is still progress if it preserves the goal logic and avoids random gap patterns
- maintaining the system is often more valuable than forcing a perfect session
- the user needs a structure that helps them re-enter without guilt or confusion

## Connection to Practice
This chapter supports a low-pressure, realistic app experience where life disruptions are part of the system instead of evidence of failure.

## Knowledge Check
What is the value of keeping a simplified version of the plan during a busy week instead of stopping entirely?
