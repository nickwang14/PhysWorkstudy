# Chapter 6: Goal-Based Progression and Deloads

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter explains how to progress training in a way that respects the user's goal, life schedule, and recovery capacity. It emphasizes reasoned progression and planned reduction phases rather than random intensity chasing.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this chapter, the learner should be able to:
- define progression in terms of consistency, load, and recovery fit
- explain how goal-based programming differs from generic training advice
- identify when a deload or reduction phase is appropriate
- adapt a plan for busy weeks without abandoning the overall goal
- understand the difference between constructive change and unnecessary disruption

## Subchapters
1. Goal-setting and training metrics
2. Progression rules
3. Deloads and reduction phases
4. Adapting to busy weeks
5. When to change the plan

## Unlock Rule
This chapter unlocks when the learner can choose a sensible progression or deload adjustment based on a realistic training week and known recovery status.

## Scenario
A learner works through a case involving chapter 6 goal based progression and deloads. They should explain what changes, why it matters, and what the next training decision should be.
