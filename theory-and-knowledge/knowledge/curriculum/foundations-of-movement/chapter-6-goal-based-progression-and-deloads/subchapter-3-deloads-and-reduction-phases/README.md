# Deloads and Reduction Phases

## Overview
Training plans need planned downtime in the system. Deloads are not optional extras; they are part of sustainable structure.

## Key Ideas
- a deload reduces load, effort, or fatigue while preserving movement quality
- it helps prevent accumulated stress from becoming a long-term problem
- deloads are often most useful after a period of repeated effort and adaptation
- reduction phases should be planned, not improvised from anxiety or frustration

## Connection to Practice
This subchapter reinforces the central product philosophy: the app will tend toward sustainable structure and consistent recovery rather than maximizing every week.
