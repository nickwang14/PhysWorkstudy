Summary

Experience suggests that an object at rest will remain at rest
if left alone, and that an object in motion tends to slow down
and stop unless some effort is made to keep it moving. What
Newton’s first law of motion states, however, is the following:

A body at rest remains at rest, or, if in motion,
remains in motion at a constant velocity unless
acted on by a net external force.
