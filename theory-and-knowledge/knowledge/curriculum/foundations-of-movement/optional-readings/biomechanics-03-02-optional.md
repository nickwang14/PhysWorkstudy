Angularvelocityisavectorquantity. Angularvelocityhasonly

Angular velocity is used in two ways in biomechanics. We
are either interests in the average angular velocity or the
instantaneous angular velocity. Average angular velocity tells
us how long it takes for something to rotate through a certain
angular displacement. Instantaneous angular velocity tells us
how fast something is spinning at a specific instant in time.
The average angular velocity of a tennis player’s swing might
determine whether or not she contacts the ball but it is the
racket’s instantaneous velocity at ball contact that determines
how fast and how far the ball will go. In sports where whole
body rotations are important (diving, gymnastics), angular
velocity is an important determinant of whether or not the
athletewillcompleteacertainnumberoftwistsorsomersaults
