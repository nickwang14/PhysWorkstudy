## Useful Non-Graphic Materials

- **Chapter Key Terms:** vocabulary verification and original glossary-card planning; indexed for all 28 chapters. Do not bulk-copy definitions.
- **Chapter Review and objectives:** scope/sequence and misconception checks; read the full source section for claims.
- **Tables in Chapter 11:** regional muscle names/actions/attachments for a structured lookup or original comparison exercise. Many tables span pages; use every `caption_occurrences` page in `index.json`, then find the table's actual start above its label.
- **Review/Critical Thinking Questions:** identify the level of reasoning expected; write original checks and verify their answers independently.
- **Interactive features:** inspect the exact linked resource and accessibility/current availability before assigning it. Do not assume a video link remains valid.
- **Alphabetical Index and References:** find related sections and underlying sources without re-searching the whole book.

## When the Index Is Enough — and When It Is Not

Use the index alone to locate material, select candidate sources or inspect recorded usage. **Return to the source** before making a new factual claim, extracting a passage/graphic, resolving disagreement, checking a technical definition, interpreting a graph, using a numerical value, changing lesson scope, or relying on a note whose source version changed. If the textbook itself is insufficient, log the question and follow its references or another evidence source; do not fabricate a source-backed answer.
