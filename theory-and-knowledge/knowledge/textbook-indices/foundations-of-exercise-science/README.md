## Limits and gaps

These passages support general reasoning about activity, movement, practice, and barriers. They do **not** establish an ideal weekly strength-training frequency, an individual prescription, a universal exercise split, or a precise recovery interval. Chapter 5 lessons use original planning cases and avoid presenting those choices as textbook mandates. Add a dedicated, validated program-design source before teaching numeric set/rep/frequency prescriptions.
