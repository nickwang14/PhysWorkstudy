# PhysiApp Decisions

## Decision Log

### PD-001 - Dual-track streak model
- Learning has a daily streak.
- Friend poke encouragement belongs only to the learning track and is deferred until post-MVP.
- Training has a weekly goal and no daily guilt-based pressure.
- Training social celebration is optional future scope, not MVP scope.

### PD-002 - Deload every 6 weeks
- Schedule a light week every 6th week.
- Preserve movement patterns while reducing training volume.

### PD-003 - Full app with editorial emphasis
- The app is not a marketing landing page.
- The curated program and academic curriculum are the core product.
- Custom logging exists as a supporting feature.
- Shareable surfaces are future product scope, not an MVP requirement.

### PD-004 - Consistency over intensity
- Rewards are based on showing up and engaging, not intensity or performance.

### PD-005 - Android primary, web companion
- Android is the main product surface.
- Flutter Web is a companion dashboard for review first.
- Shareable web surfaces are deferred until after MVP.

### PD-006 - Continuous chapter-based learning path
- The curriculum uses a program -> chapter -> subchapter -> lesson structure.
- Older year-and-term materials were removed rather than archived so the active path remains clear.
